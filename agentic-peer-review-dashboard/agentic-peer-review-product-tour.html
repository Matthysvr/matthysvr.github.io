Overview

Four product surfaces, one coherent workflow

The live app is not one screen, it is a chain of connected interfaces. This page demonstrates the highest-signal moments from each surface and preserves the actual product structure: auth, admin, participant, and trust tooling.

Auth

Admin entry

Login and registration screens gate the operational portal and route users into the admin shell.

Admin

Command center

Dashboard, employees, templates, workflows, and review detail pages handle setup and orchestration.

Participant

Conversation flow

Token-based review routes lead reviewers through the chat interview, score review, and completion states.

Trust

Audit and verification

Global audit views expose grouped events, hash continuity, payloads, and a clear integrity check workflow.

What this page emphasizes

Instead of screenshot dumping every CRUD form, the companion page focuses on the moments that best explain the product: setup, orchestration, conversation, scoring, aggregation, and audit integrity.

Mock-first, but code-grounded

Every scene here is grounded in the current routes and components, then polished into presentation-ready mock interfaces with inline motion, no external assets required.

Participant Surface

The reviewer journey stays simple and evidence-oriented

Participants do not see rubric plumbing. They receive a token link, have a natural conversation, optionally review the generated assessment, and submit. That simplicity is one of the product's strongest qualities.
