📋
Forms force reviewers into rigid boxes
Rating scales strip nuance. Reviewers game the system, grade inflate, and produce data that fails to capture real performance signals.
🎭
Bias is unchecked and undetectable
With no justification requirement, rater bias — halo effect, recency bias, affinity bias — operates invisibly. There is no way to audit a numerical score alone.
🕳️
No audit trail means no accountability
Decisions affecting careers, compensation, and promotions are made with no traceable reasoning — a legal, ethical, and operational liability.
"The form is the problem. The moment you show someone a scoring rubric, you get anchored, biased, inflated responses. The signal is drowned in the process." — Product Vision, 2025
The Solution

Conversation-First, Evidence-Backed Reviews

The form moves to the backend. Participants have a natural conversation. Agents score internally, cite evidence, and the entire process is hash-chained for auditability.

🔍

Agents Never Act Invisibly

Every score has a logged justification that cites the specific conversation content that informed it. There are no silent decisions.

🎭

Alignment is Explicit

Admins configure each agent session as Employee-Aligned, Company-Aligned, or Neutral — with optional free-text personality. No hidden defaults.

⛓️

Everything is Auditable

A SHA-256 hash chain links every state change — initial scores, participant adjustments, admin edits — into a tamper-evident audit log.

🔀

Workflows are Visual

Admins drag-and-drop a review pipeline from 7 node types — sequential, parallel, conditional — without writing a single line of code.

⛓️ Hash-Chained Audit Trail

Every state change is appended to an immutable audit chain using SHA-256. Each event references the previous hash, making the log tamper-evident.
